CLS AS-IS Process Flow Charts

Simple flow charts based on the AS-IS process mapping. Language is simplified so non-logistics and technical teams can understand the operational journey.

How to read this: Each box shows who does the work, what happens, and what document/system is usually involved. Red boxes show risk/manual pain points in the current AS-IS process.

Flow 1 — Customer Inquiry to Quotation

Customer
Sends inquiry
Customer asks for a rate or booking by email, WhatsApp, or phone.
→
Sales / CS
New or existing customer?
New customers need checks first. Existing customers move faster.
→
Sales / Finance
KYC / background check
For new customers, basic company and credit checks are done.
→
CS
Check AMS rates
CS checks if a contract or monthly rate is already available.
→
CS
Rate available?
If no rate is available, Procurement must get a rate from liner.
→
Sales
Quote customer
Sales adds markup and sends the final rate to the customer.
Current AS-IS issue: inquiries are not centrally tracked, and customer information often arrives incomplete.

Flow 2 — Rate Request and Booking Confirmation

Customer
Accepts quote
Customer confirms the rate, often by WhatsApp or email.
→
CS
Prepare booking request
CS creates an Excel booking request form.
→
Procurement
Book with liner
Procurement enters the booking in the shipping line portal.
→
Liner
Booking confirmed
The shipping line confirms space on the vessel.
→
Procurement / CS
Send release order
Customer receives permission/instructions to collect container or proceed.

Flow 3 — Cut-offs, SI, and Liner Portal Entry

Liner
Sends cut-off schedule
Deadlines for SI, BL, VGM, gate-in, etc. are received.
→
Documentation
Update Excel tracker
Cut-off dates are manually maintained in an internal Excel sheet.
→
CS
Remind customer
CS asks customer to send SI before the BL cut-off.
→
Customer
Send SI
Customer sends shipment details in Excel, Word, PDF, email, etc.
→
CS / Docs
Manual re-entry
Team reads customer SI and enters it into the liner portal.
→
Liner
BL draft generated
Liner sends a draft BL for checking and correction.
Current AS-IS issue: SI formats are not standard, and each liner portal works differently.

Flow 4 — BL Creation and Customer Approval

Direct BL shipment
SI submitted to liner
↓
Liner creates direct BL
↓
Customer checks BL draft
↓
Corrections/amendments if needed
↓
Final BL issued to customer
House / Master BL shipment
Liner issues Master BL to forwarder
↓
Forwarder creates House BL in Gensoft
↓
Customer checks House BL draft
↓
Amendments handled if needed
↓
Final House BL shared with customer
Important: BL information is often entered both in the liner portal and in Gensoft, which creates duplicate work and error risk.

Flow 5 — Cross-Trading and Switch BL

Customer / CS

→

Docs

Docs

Simple Glossary of Domain Terms

TermSimple meaning
SIShipping Instructions. The shipment details the customer gives so the shipping line can prepare the BL. It includes shipper, consignee, cargo details, packages, weight, container number, seal number, etc.
BLBill of Lading. The main shipping document. It is proof that cargo was shipped and shows who is sending, receiving, and carrying the cargo.
Draft BLA draft version of the BL sent for checking before the final BL is issued.
Direct BLA BL issued directly by the shipping line to the customer/consignee for a simple port-to-port shipment.
Master BLThe BL issued by the shipping line to the freight forwarder.
House BLThe BL issued by the freight forwarder to the customer. It is used when the forwarder manages the shipment on behalf of the customer.
Switch BLA replacement BL with changed details, often used in trading shipments to hide the original supplier from the final buyer.
Release OrderA document/instruction that allows the customer or transporter to collect an empty container or proceed with the shipment.
Cut-offThe final deadline to complete a required task before the vessel process moves forward.
SI Cut-offThe last time to submit Shipping Instructions to the liner.
BL Cut-offThe last time to finalize BL details.
VGM Cut-offThe last time to submit the verified container weight.
Gate-in / CY Cut-offThe deadline for the loaded container to enter the port/terminal.
CFS Cut-offThe deadline for loose/LCL cargo to reach the warehouse or consolidation point.
LinerThe shipping line/carrier, such as MSC, CMA, OOCL, HMM, etc.
Liner portalThe shipping line’s online system used for bookings, SI entry, BL drafts, amendments, and tracking.
AMSCLS internal pricing/customer system used mainly for rates and customer records.
GensoftInternal ERP/job system used to create shipment jobs, House BLs, documents, costing, and related records.
Spot rateA short-validity rate for a specific shipment, usually changes quickly.
Contract / Monthly rateA pre-agreed or longer-validity rate used for repeat lanes/customers.
ShipperThe party sending the cargo.
ConsigneeThe party receiving the cargo.
Destination agentThe overseas partner/agent who helps handle the shipment at destination.
Cross-tradingA shipment arranged by CLS where the cargo moves between two foreign countries without physically involving Colombo.
KYCKnow Your Customer. Basic checks done before accepting a new customer.
